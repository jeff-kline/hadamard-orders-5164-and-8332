# Statement for the first stage of the external proof review

For a prime $q$ congruent to 1 modulo 4, let $W$ be a square matrix of order $q+3$ with entries $0,1,-1$ satisfying $WW^T=(q+1)I$. The claim is that such a $W$ suffices to construct a real sign matrix $H$ of order $4(q+2)$ with $HH^T=4(q+2)I$.

First examine the statement alone. Look for a structural obstruction or an implicit restriction on the input. Use symbolic reasoning, with up to ten minutes of wall time and no matrix generation. Record what you checked, which sources you saw and any counterexample or unresolved step before opening the manuscript. A failure to find a counterexample at this stage leaves the proof to be checked.
